The Challenge

The High Cost of Manual Incident Triage

Modern microservice outages overwhelm on-call engineers with noisy telemetry, leading to costly downtime.

1. Alert Fatigue & Noise Overload

Thousands of raw logs, Sentry exceptions, and Datadog alerts bombard on-call engineers without contextual prioritization or blast radius estimation.

2. Delayed MTTA & MTTR

Engineers spend 15 to 45 minutes manually searching for wikis, inspecting stack traces, and guessing the root cause before taking action.

3. Incorrect Escalation & Team Ping-Pong

Critical incidents get escalated to the wrong team (e.g. database locks routed to web team), burning critical SLA response windows.

4. Siloed Tribal Knowledge

Past post-mortems and mitigation commands live in disorganized Notion/Confluence docs, inaccessible during high-stress outages.

The Solution

Autonomous AI Incident Triage Engine

A unified system that ingests alerts, grounds decisions in RAG runbooks, and routes tickets in sub-milliseconds.

1. Ingestion & Preprocessing

Accepts raw ticket descriptions, APM telemetry, and stack traces with automated signal extraction and noise filtering.

2. Hybrid RAG Retrieval

Combines BM25 inverted index retrieval with symptom signature boosting over 15+ verified knowledge base runbooks.

3. Dual-Engine Reasoning

Seamlessly operates in Google Gemini 2.5 Flash API mode or Local Deterministic RAG (100% offline).

4. Objective Severity Scoring

Calibrates incidents into P0 (Outage), P1 (Degraded), P2 (Moderate), and P3 (Cosmetic) with blast radius justification.

5. Intelligent Escalation Matrix

Assigns directly to 6 specialized teams with lead on-call contacts, Slack channels, and response SLA timers.

6. Actionable Remediation

Generates step-by-step mitigation checklists and 1-click copyable diagnostic CLI/kubectl/SQL terminal commands.
